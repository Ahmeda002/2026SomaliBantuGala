Faith · Our Quranic tradition

One tradition, carried forward

2022Columbus, Ohio

Ramadan Halaqa & Quran Competition

Carried the tradition forward after COVID-19. Shaped by Ahki Yussuf, Murjan Osman, Abdikadir Omar (Matir) and Brother Abdinasir of Cleveland.

2025Milwaukee

Milwaukee Quran Competition

Led by Sheikh Abdishakur Ali, with annual sign-up and a vision of uniting Somali Bantu communities across the country.

The format evolved. The purpose, Quran learning and community connection, continued.
