Welcome

Tonight, we celebrate us

Welcome to the inaugural Somali Bantu Excellence Gala, a community-led celebration of our successes and wins.

More than two decades after the first Somali Bantu families arrived in the United States, we come together to honor our graduates, builders, leaders and the families who carried them here, and to invest in the generation coming next.

Our journey

From the Jubba Valley to Louisville

1800s

Roots in East Africa

Our ancestors were brought from Mozambique, Tanzania and Malawi to Somalia’s Jubba Valley.

1991

Leaving Somalia

Civil war forced families into refugee camps in Kenya, where many spent more than a decade.

2003

A new home

The first families arrived in the U.S., part of a 12,000-person resettlement, the largest group of African refugees the U.S. had ever accepted.

2026

Excellence

Graduates, nurses, business owners and leaders in 26 states, celebrating together in Louisville.

Sources: UNHCR; Social Science Research Council; 2026 Somali Bantu Education Survey.
